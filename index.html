BP_FirstPersonCharacter

A camera that finds clues

  • Scroll on the view or drag the slider. FInterp To (speed 10) eases zoom toward the target, FOV lerps 90°→45° and walk speed drops.
  • A photo fires the flash, starts a cooldown and captures the room. A line trace counts it as evidence when it hits an Evidence actor, or a HiddenClue actor with night vision on.
  • Rooms go into a set with AddUnique. When every required room is in, the objective advances.

BP_FirstPersonCharacter · Battery

Light you have to ration

  • IA_ToggleLantern branches on Is Lantern on. Turning off hides LanternLight and clears Lantern Drain Timer. Turning on only works with charge left, then starts a looping 0.1 s timer that calls DainBattery.
  • Under Low Percent the FlickerLight Event fires. At zero the light goes out and the timer is cleared.
  • Battery pickups are children of BP_Baseinteractable: Interact adds charge, plays the pickup sound with PlaySound2D and destroys the actor.

Drain, pickup and low-battery values in the demo are example values.

BP_FirstPersonCharacter

Sanity that bends the screen

  • A looping 0.5 s timer updates sanity: +0.3 per tick in a safe zone, minus Calculate Sanity Drain in the dark, clamped, and cleared when full or empty.
  • Map Range Clamped maps sanity 100→0 to saturation 1→0 and vignette 0→2.
  • Director_Jumpscare rolls every 5 s. At 20 sanity or less: 5 or under on 1–100 is a jumpscare, otherwise 30 or under picks one of four events.

Drain rate in the demo is an example value.

BP_FakeGhostBack

A scare that waits until you look

  • The ghost spawns on a random arc behind the player with its sound placed at that spot.
  • Each check reads distance (under 500 units), Was Recently Rendered and the dot product of the player's forward vector with the direction to the ghost.
  • It keeps turning toward the player, then fires a camera-shake jumpscare.

The ghost fired at the wrong time because of linked bugs with the player Blueprint. I traced the checks one by one and fixed the visibility test.

BP_Door · BP_Key

Doors that know where you stand

  • CheckForKey tests whether the player's keys Contains the door's Key ID. Without it the door shakes.
  • With it, two timelines slide the key in and turn it, then the key hides and UnlockDoor runs.
  • The dot product of the door's right vector and the direction to the player picks the swing side, so it always opens away from you.
Bug I fixed

A fixed Delay let the door open before the key finished turning. UnlockDoor now runs from the timeline's Finished pin. Switch to "Old build" to see the bug.

BP_Combination_Lock

A lock you actually turn

  • Interacting blends to the lock's camera with Set View Target with Blend, turns off move and look input and shows the cursor.
  • A click becomes a world ray that traces the four wheels. Where it hits in the wheel's local space picks the direction.
  • RotateSelected steps the digit with modulo 10 and a WheelSpin timeline turns it. IsCodeCorrect runs after each spin.
